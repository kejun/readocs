noto_emoji_16.bff, noto_emoji_24.bff and noto_emoji_32.bff are converted
monochrome subsets of Noto Emoji, with internal codepoint remapping for
whole Emoji sequences.
Original font copyright notice: Copyright 2013 Google LLC
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see NotoEmoji-OFL.txt.

emoji.emap is generated using Unicode Emoji 17.0 data.
The Unicode data license is provided in Unicode-LICENSE.txt.

Pinned source URLs, checksums and conversion tool versions are recorded in
sources.lock.json and manifest.json. The converted resources are not
unmodified upstream font files.
